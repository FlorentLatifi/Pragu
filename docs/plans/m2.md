## Owner-implements (Florent writes, Claude reviews)

1. The Pragu score: a formula fixed in ADR-012, plus property tests. Teaches deterministic scoring and fast-check.
2. Wilson intervals in `bench/src/metrics.ts`. Teaches why 1.00 on two cases is weak evidence.
3. `pragu comment`: Markdown from a result, with a golden test. Teaches the PR-facing contract.

Plus labelling the real bench, which only Florent can do blind.

## Risks

- **Labelling time** (about 30 minutes per screen at first): start in week 1, and do not let it slip to the end.
- **Fork pull requests and code-scanning limits** may change what the action can show; the job summary is the fallback (ADR-014).
- **npm first publish** may need a manual step before OIDC (ADR-013).
